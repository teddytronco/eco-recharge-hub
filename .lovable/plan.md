# Concise three-page XD Materials redesign

## Goal
Make the site understandable within seconds while preserving the existing XD Materials logo, colors, bilingual experience, lead storage, responsive behavior, and supported claims.

## What will change

### Shared navigation and footer
- Reduce primary navigation to **Inicio**, **Servicios**, and **Nosotros**.
- Keep the language selector and a prominent **Solicitar evaluación** CTA.
- Simplify the footer to the same three destinations, direct contact details, and legal link.
- Keep mobile call, WhatsApp, and evaluation actions without overlap.

### Home
- Replace the current long page with seven concise sections: hero, three core benefits, batteries and clients served, four-step process, trust/compliance, evaluation form, and direct contact.
- Keep each section focused on one idea, with no large paragraphs and no more than three short supporting points where applicable.
- Reuse the current industrial battery photography and existing quick lead submission.
- Show the requested facility location: Ramos Arizpe, Coahuila.

### Services
- Consolidate logistics, handling, treatment/recycling coordination, documentation, materials, coverage, and compliance into four service groups.
- Limit each service to a short description and three capability bullets.
- Add the existing full inquiry form at the bottom of the page.

### About
- Reduce the main story to approximately 150–200 words across Spanish and English.
- Focus on purpose, operating region, safety/compliance approach, and accountability.
- Retain only concise trust indicators and direct contact.

### Existing URLs and SEO
- Keep `/`, `/servicios`, `/nosotros`, and `/legal` as content pages.
- Redirect obsolete top-level pages such as Contact, Process, Materials, Coverage, Compliance, Logistics, Resources, and used-battery valuation to the closest relevant section on Home or Services.
- Redirect existing detail URLs to prevent abandoned or duplicate pages.
- Update sitemap visibility and preserve unique metadata for remaining content pages.

## Technical details
- Add a compact reusable inquiry form that uses the current lead-saving flow and requested fields.
- Keep the full existing form logic available through the consolidated Services page.
- Use semantic design tokens and existing XD assets; no new palette or logo treatment.
- Validate Spanish and English at desktop, tablet, and mobile sizes, including navigation, forms, redirects, and contact actions.

## Content constraints
- Do not invent permits, certifications, partners, processing capabilities, prices, or client names.
- Do not mention Redwood or imply exclusivity.
- Keep detailed compliance wording inside one compact expandable disclosure.
- Use the current verified contact email (`crecio@xd-global.com`) and phone numbers; the older email in the brief will not replace verified current contact data.
